Day 21     April 9, 1980  Wednesday
USGS daily report
Cloud cover prevented scientists from observing the mountain for most of today except early, either from the air or the ground, but light ashfalls near Timberline Viewpoint and Spirit Lake and the odor of sulfur marked at least three eruptions. Tuesday's apparent decrease in seismic activity continued today. Yesterday, only 38 earthquakes greater than magnitude 3 were recorded, four of which reached or exceeded magnitude 4. Today's totals were 37 and six, respectively. No harmonic tremor was recorded either day. [This general level of seismic activity was to continue throughout April. Most days in April saw less than 40 events stronger than magnitude 3 and less than 10 events of magnitude 4 or greater.] The new tiltmeter installed near Timberline Viewpoint started sending continuous records to Vancouver that are expected to show any deformation of the mountain's northern flank.   [USGS PP 1249]

Historic photographs
The 1920 black & white photograph of Mount St. Helens from Timberline is a great shot, illustrating how the volcano's avalanche-prone, nearly-barren slopes kept a ski area from ever being built (even though ski enthusiasts kept trying). You can see the Forsyth Glacier with the three very prominent features on the north flank - the Dog’s Head, Sugar Bowl, and Goat Rocks.
